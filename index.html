Our Menu

Venezuelan Street Food Favorites

Everything is made fresh, full of flavor, and authentically Venezuelan. Order online for carryout today.

Cachapas

Thick, sweet corn pancakes made fresh from ground maize, filled with soft white cheese. A true Venezuelan comfort food — crispy outside, soft and gooey inside.

Must Try

Pastelitos

Flaky, golden Venezuelan pastries filled with seasoned chicken, beef, or cheese. Baked to perfection with a crispy crust and a savory, juicy filling — impossible to eat just one.

Fan Favorite

Almuerzos

Venezuela's national dish — shredded beef (carne mechada), black beans, white rice, and sweet fried plantains (tajadas). A full plate of Venezuelan soul in every bite.

Classic

Pastel del Papa

A rich and comforting Venezuelan potato pastry, savory and satisfying. One of our most beloved traditional dishes.

Traditional

Morocho

Traditional Venezuelan bread made with a blend of corn and wheat flour — dense, slightly sweet, and irresistibly soft inside with a golden crust. A Venezuelan bakery staple enjoyed at any time of day.
