Our Story

Real Venezuelan Street Food in Dallas

At Tu Pastelito.com, we bring the authentic taste of Venezuelan street food straight to Dallas, Texas. Located in Far North Dallas at 18110 Midway Rd, Suite #104 — between Midway Rd and Frankford Rd — we're your go-to destination for Venezuelan classics.

From our crispy Tequeños and hearty Empanadas to fresh Arepas, Pastel del Papa, and warm Morocho, every item on our menu is made with the same passion and tradition that Venezuelan street food is known for.

We're open every day from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and we offer convenient online ordering for carryout.

What We Stand For

Authentic Flavors, Every Day

Venezuelan street food is bold, comforting, and deeply rooted in tradition. We honor those roots in everything we make — from the way we prepare our Tequeños to how we fill each Arepa.

We serve Venezuelan, Latin American, and Breakfast options — a little something for everyone, any time of day.
